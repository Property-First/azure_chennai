Welcome To Godrej Azure

Godrej Chennai rises as the crown jewel of Padur’s OMR, weaving a serene coastal charm with refined urban living. Spanning 8.77 acres, Godrej Azure unveils six graceful towers offering 2 & 3 BHK residences—each thoughtfully planned with just four corner units per floor, 68% carpet efficiency, and up to 50% panoramic sea views. A 20,000 sq. ft. grand clubhouse, 30+ lifestyle indulgences, IGBC Gold pre-certification, and 70% verdant open spaces make Godrej Chennai a harmonious blend of elegance and sustainability. Perfectly positioned near Siruseri IT Park, the upcoming Metro, Muttukadu backwaters, and premier schools, Godrej Chennai enjoys seamless connectivity through ECR and key city arteries. Surrounded by vibrant malls, gourmet avenues, and reputed healthcare, Godrej Azure promises a lifestyle both enchanting and effortless - where sea breezes, lush landscapes, and a lively community craft a world of coastal sophistication.
